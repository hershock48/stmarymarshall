# The research ledger

Every piece of research behind the diocese pitch, what it found, what was
decided, and where it stands. Read this before reviewing or editing a
letter, the agreement or the plan. If a finding is not in a letter, the
reason is written here.

Written 2026-11-05, after Kevin caught a review that measured the letters
against an order the later research had replaced. The rule since: a newer
ruling or note supersedes an older one, and the row here says which.

Status words: **in the letters**, **in the agreement**, **built**, **not
built**, **dropped** (with why), **Kevin's** (his fact or his step).

## 4. Who owns what

`glaze/research/narthex-ownership-2026-11.md`.

| Finding | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Data, content, domains and accounts are the diocese's outright; export at any time | Adopted | In the letters and agreement. Export is by hand within ten days; self-serve is **not built**. |
| An exit license on objective triggers, with a quarterly deposit | Adopted, and worded as what the diocese holds | In the agreement as "Continuity". The deposit script and its attestation are **not built**; they start at go-live. |
| Source-available publication | Not yet | Revisit at three or more dioceses. |
| Stripe: accounts with full Dashboard, direct charges, no platform controls | The only way the donor promise is true | In the agreement. Giving is **not built**; build it this way or the promise breaks. |
| Diocesan policies elsewhere put domains in the Church's own name | Cite it | In the Kalamazoo letter, three questions. |
| A product entity that owns the platform by IP assignment; attorney redraft; insurance | Before the first diocese signs | **Kevin's**, in the plan's to-do with costs. |
| What a diocese asks a vendor for: W-9, certificate of insurance, a second person | Supply them | **Kevin's.** Not in the letters. |

## What a letter promises that the platform cannot yet do

Keep it true. As of 2026-11-05, not built: giving through the platform,
hosted video, the office panel, texting, the self-serve export, the
quarterly deposit script, the uptime monitor. Nothing is billed before it
runs.
