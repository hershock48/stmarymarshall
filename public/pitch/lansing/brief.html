For the Diocese of Lansing
November 2026

Seventy-three parishes. One living platform.

Connected to each other, and to the diocese. Its Mass finder covers all 73 Lansing parishes today, and three parishes run on it in the Diocese of Kalamazoo.

The offer, in three steps

1This month, free, nothing signedA finder of the diocese’s own and the bishop’s voice. Ninety days; nothing renews on its own.The ask: Point the find-a-parish page on dioceseoflansing.org at it.
2Any parish you name, built freeThree run on it now in the Diocese of Kalamazoo; St. Philip, Battle Creek is the one to open.The ask: Name as many as you like and we build them all.
3Then one licenseAll 73 parishes included, moving at their own pace. Each old subscription ends after its site is live.

Between parishes

Connected to each other

  • The gatherings of every parish on one page, as parishes join
  • Every group and ministry, with the person to call
  • One Mass finder for all 73 parishes, from the diocese’s own directory
  • Each parish that joins adds its Mass times the same day

For the diocese

A way to see, and to send

  • The bishop’s voice on every parish home page, with his podcast already in it
  • A notice typed once, on every parish site
  • The weekly email compiled from the parish calendars
  • Every site’s health and use on one page, by vicariate

For a parish

A site that is alive

  • The next Mass, Confession and Adoration, worked out from the clock
  • The holy day on every page the week before
  • The week’s news typed once and printed as the bulletin insert
  • A calendar anyone can subscribe to, and Watch at Mass time
  • The office texts a change and it is up the same week

What it costs

$4,990 a month

One line to the diocese. All 73 parishes included. No parish is billed.

No build fee. Twelve months, then month to month on thirty days’ notice.

$59,880 a year, about one percent of this year’s appeal goal.

Giving through the platform, once built: 1% of a gift, capped at $5, on the parish’s own Stripe account.

What the diocese owns

Every page, photo, schedule, calendar and form. Its domains, in its own registrar account. Each parish’s giving account, with every donor in it. Export at any time, free, as files, as data and as a copy of each site that runs on any host.

The diocese also holds the platform itself: a sealed copy, source included, delivered every quarter, with a written license to run it for its own parishes.

eCatholic’s terms, for the twenty-six parishes on it, say it “will not provide you with any form of export of your Website.”
